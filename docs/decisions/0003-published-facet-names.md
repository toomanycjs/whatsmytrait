# 0003: published facet names in the IPIP-NEO-120

Date: 3 November 2026. Made while building, for the owner to confirm or overturn.

The brief approves the IPIP-NEO-120 as a whole and bans clinical words from the site. Two of its thirty facets are named by the authors Anxiety (N1) and Depression (N3). They are facets of the Neuroticism domain: four-item trait scales about how readily worry and low mood arise, not screeners for any condition.

Decision: keep the authors' names in the data file, since the brief also requires instruments to be named as their authors name them, and show those names on the results page and the facet pages. The wordlist check allows exactly those two name lines in a data file and nothing else; the words stay banned in the site's own copy, so no page describes anyone with them. Every page that shows a facet score says that it describes a tendency and is not a diagnosis.

If the owner would rather not show these names, the alternative is to show the facet strips under the domain without naming N1 and N3, and to say on the methodology page why two facets are unnamed. That would be a visible gap a psychology lecturer would notice, so it is not the default.
