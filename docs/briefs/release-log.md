# Release log

One entry per release from `docs/briefs/next-releases.md` (content) and `docs/briefs/new-tests.md` (tests): the date, the pages added or changed, and the searches each change targets, so the next Search Console review can compare before and after.

## 10 November 2026: fixes from the content brief

Changed: every content and trust page now shows Published and Updated dates; bylines "By the What's My Trait team"; /about/ (How this site is made); /licences/ (The norm datasets); /methodology/ (age bands, Last reviewed); /traits/anxiety/ and /traits/depression/ return 301.

Searches targeted: none directly; trust and freshness signals across the site.

## 10 November 2026: test release T1, Am I a perfectionist?

Added: /tests/perfectionism/ (IPIP-HEXACO perfectionism facet, 10 items, alpha .72).

Changed: /guides/orderliness-and-perfectionism/ (a sentence linking to the test); /traits/orderliness/ (a sentence linking to the test); /tests/ (listed under "How do I compare on one trait"); /tests/patience/ and /guides/patience-as-a-trait/ (alpha corrected from .88 to .80, the IPIP scale's own figure); /licences/, /methodology/ and /for-teachers-and-researchers/ list the new instrument.

Searches targeted: am I a perfectionist, perfectionism test, perfectionist test, am I a perfectionist quiz, perfectionism personality trait, perfectionism vs orderliness.

## 10 November 2026: test releases T2, T5, T6 and part of T7

Added: /tests/empathy/ (IPIP empathy scale similar to the JPI-R, 10 items, alpha .80); /tests/leadership/ (IPIP-VIA leadership, 7 items, alpha .78); /tests/optimism/ (IPIP optimism scale similar to Scheier, Carver and Bridges, 10 items, alpha .86); /tests/need-for-cognition/ (10 items, alpha .84); /tests/self-monitoring/ (10 items, alpha .82). Locus of control, the third T7 test, waits for the owner's decision on its scales.

Changed: a sentence linking to the new tests on /traits/sympathy/, /traits/assertiveness/, /traits/cheerfulness/, /traits/intellect/, /traits/extraversion/, /tests/emotional-intelligence/ and /tests/character-strengths/; /tests/ gains a "For coursework" group.

Searches targeted: empathy test, how empathetic am I, emotional empathy test; am I a leader, leadership test, leadership quiz; optimism test, am I an optimist or a pessimist; need for cognition test, need for cognition scale; self-monitoring test, self-monitoring scale Snyder.

## 10 November 2026: test release T4, Am I shy?

Added: /tests/shyness/ (IPIP social discomfort scale similar to the TCI, HA3, 10 items, alpha .86 in 501 adults of the Eugene-Springfield Community Sample; a higher score means shyer). Chosen by the owner.

Changed: /guides/introversion-is-not-shyness/ and /tests/introvert-or-extrovert/ each gain a sentence linking to the test.

Searches targeted: am I shy, shyness test, how shy am I, shy or introvert, shyness quiz.

## 10 November 2026: test release T7, locus of control

Added: /tests/locus-of-control/ (IPIP Total Locus of Control scale keyed to Levenson, 1981: internality minus powerful others and chance, 20 items, alpha .86 in the Eugene-Springfield Community Sample; a higher score means more internal). The owner chose the total over the three subscales.

Changed: /traits/self-efficacy/ and /tests/need-for-cognition/ each gain a sentence linking to the test; listed under "For coursework" on /tests/.

Searches targeted: locus of control test, internal or external locus of control, locus of control quiz, Levenson locus of control scale.
