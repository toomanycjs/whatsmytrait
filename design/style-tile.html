Introvert or extrovert?

IPIP Extraversion scale, 20 items, 3 minutes

Extraversion is the tendency to seek company, conversation and stimulation. This scale asks how accurately twenty short statements describe you, scores them on your device, and shows where you fall among 3,467 adults who answered the same items.
